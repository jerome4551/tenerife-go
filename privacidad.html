Privacy Policy

Tenerife Go — Last updated: October 2026

1. Data controller

Jérôme Bourgeois · tenerife.go.app@gmail.com · Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain.

2. What data is processed

  • Location: only when you turn it on, to show you where you are on the map, what is near you and the route to a place. It is not stored on any server. If you ask for a driving route, the start and end coordinates are sent to the routing service to work it out.
  • Account (optional): email address and password. They are handled by Supabase, which stores the password encrypted; we never see it.
  • Favourites: the identifiers of the places you save. With an account they sync; without one they stay on your device.
  • Preferences and usage: language, filters, active bus lines, cached weather and sea conditions, whether you have seen the tour, a visit counter, your answer to the cookie notice and, for the sky, the red light, the compass adjustment and the Wikipedia summaries of the objects you have already opened, the calendar reminders you mark and whether you have seen the compass guide. The events you add to your calendar (.ics) and the pictures you save or share are created on the phone and go straight to your calendar or to the system share sheet. All of this stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn them on, the subscription your browser creates — a delivery address and two keys — is stored, together with the app’s language, so the alert can reach you in your language. Turning them off in the browser stops it being used.
  • Phone orientation (optional): only while you use “Point at the sky”: the compass, gyroscope and accelerometer say where you are pointing, to name what is in front of you. It is used on the device and is neither stored nor sent.
  • Camera (optional): only if you turn it on in “Point at the sky”, to draw the stars over the picture. The picture is not stored, not analysed and never leaves the phone; only if you tap 📸 is a capture composed with the stars over it, which is saved or shared from your phone without going through any server.
  • Usage statistics: only on the website, and only if you accept them. See the next section.

3. Statistics and cookies

On the website, the app uses Google Analytics 4, and it is not loaded until you accept the cookie notice: until then nothing leaves the app towards Google.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets its own cookies and receives browsing data — screens viewed, language, approximate country, device type — and your IP address.

It runs with Consent Mode v2 and the advertising signals stay denied at all times, so that data is not used to personalise ads. If you decline, nothing is loaded.

Your answer is kept on your device for 180 days; after that you are asked again. To change it sooner, clear the site data in your browser.

The App Store app does not use Google Analytics: it never loads it and does not show the cookie notice.

4. Advertising and promoted content

The map shows businesses that pay a monthly promotion plan. They are marked with the label «Sponsored» and do not alter the rest of the content.

That promotion does not use your data: everyone is shown the same ones, with no targeting, no profiling and no advertising cookies. There is no third-party ad network running in the app.

5. Third parties that receive data

To work, the app requests data from outside services. They all receive your IP address and, in addition:

  • Supabase — account, favourites and notification subscription.
  • OpenStreetMap · ArcGIS — the map tiles, which is to say which area you are looking at.
  • OSRM — start and end points when you calculate a driving route.
  • Nominatim — the text you type when searching for an address.
  • Open-Meteo — the coordinates of the place or area whose forecast you are checking.
  • Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica y el Reto Demográfico — the official petrol station prices for the province, when you look at them; nothing of yours is sent to it.
  • Wikipedia · Wikimedia — the article title of the place or object you open.
  • Google Analytics — only on the website, if you accept the cookie notice.

None of this data is sold or passed on for commercial purposes.

6. Purpose and legal basis

  • Providing the interactive tourist guide service (legitimate interest / performance of a contract).
  • Location, notifications and statistics: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

7. Retention

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. You can delete it whenever you like, along with your favourites, from the app («Account» → «Delete account») or by email. How to do it: Delete your account

Anything stored on your device disappears when you clear the site data or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction and objection. Exercise them by writing to tenerife.go.app@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the AEPD.

9. Security

All communication goes over HTTPS. Supabase stores passwords encrypted and table access is restricted by RLS; we have no access to your password.

10. Contact

tenerife.go.app@gmail.com

En la web, la app usa Google Analytics 4, y no se carga hasta que acepta el aviso de cookies: hasta entonces no sale nada hacia Google desde la app.

Su respuesta se guarda 180 días en su dispositivo; después se vuelve a preguntar. Para cambiarla antes, borre los datos del sitio en su navegador.

La aplicación de la App Store no usa Google Analytics: no lo carga nunca y no enseña el aviso de cookies.
